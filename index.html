The Vancouver Life Drawing Society. Not-for-profit, volunteer-run, ten sessions a week. Everyone is welcome, from beginners to seasoned artists!

The weekly schedule

  • Monday Evening, 6 pm to 9 pm: Regular drawing session. 1, 2, 5 & 10 minute warm ups, three ½ hour poses.
  • Tuesday Morning, 10 am to 1 pm: Regular drawing session. 1, 2, 5 & 10 minute warm ups, three ½ hour poses.
  • Tuesday Evening, 6 pm to 9 pm: Portrait drawing session. A clothed, seated, 3-hour pose.
  • Wednesday Evening, 7 pm to 10 pm: Gestures. 2 to 5 minute poses.
  • Thursday Morning, 10 am to 1 pm: Regular drawing session. A changing variety of short and medium gestures, followed by several longer poses.
  • Thursday Evening, 7 pm to 10 pm: Regular drawing session. 1, 2, 5 & 10 minute warm ups, three ½ hour poses.
  • Friday Evening, 7 pm to 10 pm: Long-pose / Multiday session. Same pose for three hours.
  • Saturday Morning, 10 am to 1 pm: Regular drawing session. 1, 2, 5 & 10 minute warm ups, then several longer poses.
  • Sunday Morning, 10 am to 1 pm: Long-pose session. 30 minutes of warm-ups followed by a single two-hour pose.
  • Sunday Afternoon, 2 pm to 5 pm: Gestures. ½, 1, 2 and 5 minute poses for three hours.
